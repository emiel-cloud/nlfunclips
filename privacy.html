Privacy Policy

NL Fun Clips Uploader · Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy explains how the NL Fun Clips Uploader ("the app") handles information. The app is a private tool used only by the owner of the NL Fun Clips accounts (@nlfunclips).

What we collect

When the owner logs in with TikTok, TikTok gives the app an access token, a refresh token and the account's open ID, limited to the permissions the owner approved (video upload and basic profile info). The app does not collect information about other TikTok users, viewers or followers.

How we use it

The tokens are used only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts and to check the upload status. The app never posts publicly on its own: the owner reviews and publishes every draft in the TikTok app.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored only on the owner's private working environment, are never published, sold or shared with third parties, and are deleted when the owner disconnects the app.

Your choices

You can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. After revocation the app can no longer upload anything.
